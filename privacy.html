Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This policy describes how "The Billion Bot Uploader" (the "App"), an internal tool operated by The Billion Bot YouTube channel, handles data.

What the App does

The App is used only by the owner of The Billion Bot channel to manage that channel through the YouTube Data API: uploading and scheduling its own videos, setting thumbnails, adding captions and translations, managing playlists, and reading and replying to comments on its own videos. It is not offered to the public and does not access any other user's account.

Data accessed

With the channel owner's authorization (Google OAuth), the App accesses the owner's YouTube channel data needed for the tasks above, such as video metadata, captions, playlists, public comments on the channel's videos and basic channel statistics.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to perform the channel management tasks listed above.
  • OAuth tokens are stored locally on the channel owner's computer and are never shared.
  • No data is sold, rented or shared with third parties, and no data is used for advertising.
  • Public comments read by the App are used only to respond to viewers on our own videos.

Google API Services

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The App uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Data retention and deletion

The App does not keep a database of YouTube data. Information read from the API (for example video statistics or comments) is used immediately for the task and is not stored beyond temporary local working files, which are deleted within 30 days. OAuth tokens stay on the owner's computer until access is revoked; revoking access (see below) invalidates them, and they are then deleted from the computer. To request deletion of any data related to you, contact us via the channel link below and we will delete it within 7 days.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account security settings: myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Visitors to this website

This website does not use cookies, analytics or tracking. It is hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may process basic technical data (such as IP addresses) as described in its own privacy statement.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the contact details on our YouTube channel: youtube.com/@TheBillionBot.
